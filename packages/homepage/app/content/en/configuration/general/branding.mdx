## Branding options

Issuary authentication screens can use a custom logo and localized service name.

```yaml
branding:
  icon_url: https://example.com/logo.png

  title:
    en: My App
    ko: 내 앱
    ja: マイアプリ
```

- `icon_url`: URL for the icon or logo shown on authentication pages and other branded interfaces. The recommended size is 128×128 pixels.
- `title`: Localized application title keyed by ISO 639-1 language code, such as `en`, `ko`, or `ja`.
