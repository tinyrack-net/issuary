## Branding options

Issuary uses the official app icon by default. You can customize the app icon,
replace the complete title treatment with a logo, and localize the login copy.

```yaml
branding:
  icon_url: https://example.com/icon.svg
  logo_url: https://example.com/logo.svg

  title:
    en: My App
    ko: 내 앱
    ja: マイアプリ

  subtitle:
    en: Nice to meet you!
    ko: 만나서 반가워요!
    ja: はじめまして！

  login_method_description:
    en: Choose how you'd like to sign in.
    ko: 원하는 방식으로 로그인하세요.
    ja: お好きな方法でログインしてください。
```

- `icon_url`: App icon URL. It replaces the official Issuary icon in the UI, favicon, and Apple Touch Icon.
- `logo_url`: Optional logo URL that replaces the icon and text title together on authentication pages. The configured title remains the accessible name.
- `title`: Localized application title keyed by ISO 639-1 language code, such as `en`, `ko`, or `ja`.
- `subtitle`: Localized subtitle on `/login` and `/login/password`.
- `login_method_description`: Localized guidance shown only on the login method selection screen.

Set `subtitle` or `login_method_description` to an empty string for a locale to hide that text.
